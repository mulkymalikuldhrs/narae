## Image Specifications

### Photography Style
- **Aesthetic:** Clean, minimal, luxury
- **Lighting:** Soft, natural, diffused light
- **Backgrounds:** Pure white (#FFFFFF), Cream (#F5F0E8), or Soft Lavender gradient
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1200px width
- **Aspect Ratios:** 16:9 or 4:3
- **Shadows:** Soft, subtle shadow beneath product

### Product Shot Guidelines

**Mini Sachet (30g):**
- Show front of sachet with logo clearly visible
- Clean white background
- Straight-on or slight angle
- Optional: Back side showing ingredients

**Medium Pouch (100g):**
- Standing position at 45-degree angle
- Show pouch shape and branding
- White or cream background
- Optional: Open pouch showing product texture

**Premium Set (250g):**
- Complete gift set composition (jar + wooden spoon + box)
- Soft lavender gradient background
- Premium positioning with props
- Detail shots of texture and spoon

### Hero/Lifestyle Images

**Requirements:**
- Setting: Bathroom, bedroom, or spa-like environment
- Props: Minimal - towels, plants, candles, natural elements
- Mood: Calm, serene, luxurious
- Models (if used): Natural, relaxed poses
- Color palette: Matches brand colors (neutral, lavender, gold)
- Optional overlay: Subtle gradient (black 10% opacity at bottom)

## Brand Colors for Backgrounds

| Color Name | Hex Code | Usage |
|------------|----------|-------|
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary product backgrounds |
| Cream | `#F5F0E8` | Secondary backgrounds |
| Soft Lavender | `#E8DFE8` | Premium product backgrounds |

## Optimization Guidelines

- **Primary format:** WebP with JPEG fallback
- **Icons:** SVG format
- **Loading:** Lazy loading for below-fold images
- **Placeholder:** Use blur-up technique during load
- **Max file size:** 200KB per image
- **Quality:** 90% for JPEG/WebP

## Technical Notes

- All images must be high resolution (min 1200px width)
- Maintain consistent lighting across all product photos
- Use soft, diffused natural light when possible
- Shadows should be soft and subtle, not harsh
- Keep backgrounds clean and uncluttered
- Ensure logo and text on packaging is clearly readable
- Images should match the luxury aesthetic of the brand
